## What happened

Review commit `fae3d4eeec55` (line numbers in the review body were hallucinated; files are ~313 / ~637 lines). Two findings, both valid on HEAD.

P1: `parseScribeResponse` passed `new_items` through; `newBacklogItem` interpolated `item.slug` into `.context/backlog/items/${slug}.md` and `mutate` wrote via `join(cwd(), path)` with no allowlist or containment. Absolute slugs and `../` segments could write outside `.context/`.

Fidelity (user comparison of `.context/2026-08-26.deterministic-bsave/` vs `.context/2026-08-26.b-commit-placeholder-sentinels/`):

- b-save-improved wrote a 4-line frontmatter stub and `applyLoose` vacuumed every `.context/*.md` into the subject (`Research_Memory_Index.md`, `_QRPRO.md`, `backlog.md`).
- Memory narrative lacked the b-save section set; artifacts/related listed implementation source.

## Decision

Fix in-session (2 valid findings + mechanical fidelity defects; ≤8 files). Shared write-gate in `save-apply` rather than call-site patches. Scribe parse drops bad slugs; apply still fail-closes. Loose artifacts are session-shaped names only. Subject index gets topics/memory and fills an empty body; existing bodies are not clobbered.
