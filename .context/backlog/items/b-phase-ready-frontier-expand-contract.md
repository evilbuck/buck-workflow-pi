# b-phase ready-frontier + expand–contract

Upstream `mattpocock/skills` phasing concepts: a **ready frontier** (which phases of a phased plan are unblocked and ready to execute next) plus **expand–contract** (expand one phase into finer steps when it proves too large, contract related steps back into a single phase when the split was wrong).

Deferred from the 2026-09-10 mattpocock/skills adoption plan ([plan-mattpocock-findings-remediation.md](../../2026-09-10.mattpocock-adoption/plan-mattpocock-findings-remediation.md), Tier 4): core phased execution (`b-phase`, `b-loop` stamping) already works without this; the frontier and expand–contract are scheduling polish, not a capability hole. Not on the audit's rejected list — a genuine future enhancement.
